WHY BOTH GROUPS ARE IN ONE PICTURE.

Any plant from the bottom rows gets placed on the soil of any pot from the top rows. So they must share one camera angle, one light, one scale, one level of detail. Draw them as one set.

STYLE.

Flat vector. Solid fills, at most two tones per surface — a lit tone and a shade tone. No gradients, no photographic texture, no painterly brushwork, no glossy highlights.

Pale and bright. Low saturation, high value. Fresh gentle greens, not deep or vivid.

Simple shapes. A plant is six to nine clear leaf shapes, not forty. Every item reads at thumbnail size.

Isometric, tilted so a circle on the ground appears exactly twice as wide as it is tall — the orange ellipses already show that angle. Every pot and every plant uses it.

Light comes from the upper left.
