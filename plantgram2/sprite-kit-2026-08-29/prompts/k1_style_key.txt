Style, and this matters more than the content:
- FLAT VECTOR. Solid fills, at most two tones per surface: a lit tone and a shade tone. No gradients, no texture, no speckling, no watercolour, no painterly brushwork, no photographic detail, no glossy highlights.
- BRIGHT AND AIRY. Keep everything light — pale, clean, high-key. Nothing dark or heavy. The greens are fresh and gentle, not deep or vivid.
- SIMPLE SHAPES. A plant is six to nine clear leaf shapes, not forty. Every object must read instantly at the size of a thumbnail. Silhouette first, detail almost never.
- Soft even daylight, no strong shadows. One faint soft shadow under each object.
- Isometric: every single item — the greenhouse, the tiles, the pots — is drawn at the SAME camera angle, tilted so a square on the ground appears twice as wide as it is tall.
